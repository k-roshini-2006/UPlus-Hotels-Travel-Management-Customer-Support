# U+ Hotels Travel Management Customer Support (TMCS)

## Project Overview

U+ Hotels Travel Management Customer Support (TMCS) is a hospitality service management application developed using Pega Platform. The application is designed to streamline guest support operations, manage hotel partner onboarding, and improve customer service through intelligent case management.

The application provides a centralized platform for handling guest complaints, partner onboarding, reservations, service requests, guest profiles, and hotel partner information.

## Objectives

- Improve guest complaint resolution
- Streamline hotel partner onboarding
- Provide centralized guest and reservation information
- Enable intelligent case routing and escalation
- Support automated guest recognition
- Recommend appropriate service recovery actions
- Integrate with external hospitality systems

## Key Features

## Workflows

### 1. Guest Complaint

The Guest Complaint workflow manages complaints raised by hotel guests.

Typical flow:

Guest Complaint → Investigation → Resolution → Closure

### 2. Partner Onboarding

The Partner Onboarding workflow manages the registration and approval of new hotel partners.

Typical flow:

Partner Registration → Verification → Approval → Partner Activation

### 3. Issue Management

The Issue Management workflow helps customer support teams track and resolve service-related issues.
