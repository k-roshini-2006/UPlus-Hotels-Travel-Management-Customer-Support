
# U+ Hotels Travel Management Customer Support (TMCS)

## Overview

U+ Hotels Travel Management Customer Support (TMCS) is a
hospitality service management application developed using
Pega Platform.

The application helps manage guest complaints, hotel partner
onboarding, reservations, service requests, and customer support
operations.

## Key Features

- Guest Complaint Management
- Partner Onboarding
- Guest Profile Management
- Reservation Management
- Service Request Management
- Intelligent Case Routing
- Issue Management
- Customer Support Workflows
- Hotel Partner Management

## Workflows

1. Complaint Resolution
2. Partner Onboarding
3. Issue Management

## Technology

- Pega Platform
- Pega App Studio
- Pega Blueprint
- Workflow Automation
- Case Management

## Project Architecture

The application contains multiple personas, workflows,
and data objects designed for hospitality customer support.
